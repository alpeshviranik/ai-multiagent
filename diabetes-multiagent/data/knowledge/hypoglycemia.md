# Hypoglycemia

## What it is
Hypoglycemia means low blood sugar, usually below 70 mg/dL (3.9 mmol/L). It can happen with insulin or certain diabetes pills, missed meals, extra exercise, or alcohol.

## Symptoms
Shakiness, sweating, fast heartbeat, hunger, dizziness, headache, irritability, blurry vision, and confusion. Severe lows can cause seizures or loss of consciousness.

## The 15-15 rule
If you are awake and able to swallow and your sugar is below 70 mg/dL: eat or drink 15 grams of fast-acting carbohydrate (such as 4 oz of juice, glucose tablets, or regular soda). Recheck in 15 minutes. Repeat if still below 70. Once back to normal, have a snack or meal. Tell your care team about frequent lows.
