# Hyperglycemia and DKA

## High blood sugar
Hyperglycemia means blood sugar above your target range. Signs include increased thirst, frequent urination, tiredness, and blurry vision. Causes include illness, stress, missed medication, and eating more carbohydrates than planned.

## Diabetic ketoacidosis warning signs
DKA is a serious emergency, most common in type 1 diabetes. Warning signs include nausea or vomiting, stomach pain, fruity-smelling breath, fast deep breathing, extreme tiredness, and confusion. Seek emergency care immediately.

## Sick day basics
Illness can raise blood sugar. Keep taking prescribed medicines unless your care team says otherwise, drink plenty of sugar-free fluids, check blood sugar more often, and call your care team if you cannot keep fluids down or have ketones.
